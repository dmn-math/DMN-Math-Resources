Why do all three properties hold?

Compare triangles OAP and OBP. Both are right-angled because a radius is perpendicular to a tangent at the point of contact. They share hypotenuse OP, and OA = OB because both are radii.

So △OAP ≅ △OBP by RHS (right angle, hypotenuse, side). Corresponding sides and angles are equal, giving PA = PB, ∠APO = ∠OPB, and ∠AOP = ∠POB.
